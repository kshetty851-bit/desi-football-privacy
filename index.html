Desi Football Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Desi Football ("the game") is a football game for Android by Chai Break Studios. This policy explains what information the game uses.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. We do not ask you to create an account, and we never receive your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, location or anything about how you play.

Ads

If you have not bought anything, the game shows one full-screen ad after each match, served by Google AdMob. To show ads (and, only if you agree, personalised ads), Google may use your device's advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and information about the ads you see and tap. This goes directly from your phone to Google, not to us. Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent first using Google's consent form, and you can change your choice later. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Buying any unlock in the game removes all ads permanently.

Information stored on your phone

The game saves your settings, trophies, and your Sarpanch Cup and Gaon League progress on your own device so it can remember them next time. This stays on your phone, is never sent to us, and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Purchases

Desi Football is free to download, with matches up to 5 minutes free. Longer matches (10 and 15 minutes) can be unlocked with one-time in-app purchases on Google Play. Any purchase also removes all ads for good. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play: we never see your card, UPI or bank details. The game asks Google Play which unlocks your Google account owns, so it can turn them on, including after a reinstall; this check happens between your phone and Google Play and nothing is sent to us. See the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
