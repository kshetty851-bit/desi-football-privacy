Desi Football Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Desi Football ("the game") is a football game for Android by Chai Break Studios. This policy explains what information the game uses.

Information we collect

None. The game does not connect to the internet. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, location or anything about how you play.

Information stored on your phone

The game saves your settings, trophies, and your Sarpanch Cup and Gaon League progress on your own device so it can remember them next time. This stays on your phone, is never sent to us, and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Buying the game

Desi Football is a one-time purchase on Google Play. There are no ads and no in-app purchases. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play: we never see your card, UPI or bank details. See the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
